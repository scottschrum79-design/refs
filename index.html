U8 General Rules

  • Games are divided into four 10-minute quarters.
  • Players may be substituted only at the halfway point of each quarter, except in the case of an injury.
  • The referee will stop the game clock around the five-minute mark of each quarter for substitutions, if desired.
  • A player may play goalkeeper for no more than one quarter. Goalkeepers may be changed only between quarters.
  • Halftime is five minutes.
  • Games are played five versus five, plus a goalkeeper for each team.
  • A suggested formation is three offensive players and two defensive players. Keeping those players primarily on their respective halves of the field encourages passing and more touches.
  • All penalty kicks are indirect.
  • Goalkeepers may not punt the ball.
  • Heading is not permitted.
  • On a goal kick, the opposing team must return to its own half of the field until the ball is put into play.
